CardScanFox is an AI-powered trading card scanner and collection management platform that helps collectors identify trading cards, explore card prices, and manage their collections across multiple TCG and sports card categories.

SCAN WITH CONTROL
Capture a card or choose photos from your library. Card images are cropped and hashed on your device. The cropped card area is uploaded to CardScanFox's private service for scan records and recognition-quality review, retained without a fixed expiration period, and never sent to the external recognition provider. Check the set, full collector number, language, and finish before saving a suggested match to your collection.

ORGANIZE YOUR COLLECTION
Track quantities, conditions, grading details, purchase values, and notes. Use folders to keep different parts of your portfolio organized, and save cards to your Wishlist for later.

FOLLOW VALUE
Check the dates on available reference prices and view price history and changes to your portfolio in your selected currency. Card and price information may be incomplete or delayed and is provided for collection management, not financial advice.

USE IT YOUR WAY
Start as a guest or sign in to keep an account. Manage preferences, contact support, review the Terms and Privacy Policy, and delete your account from within the app.
