Questions.

What is my card worth?

Scan a card and CardScanFox suggests likely matches from the catalog. Review the set and card number, confirm the finish and language, then choose a raw condition or enter the grade shown on an existing slab to see the relevant market value.

What should I look for in a trading card scanner app?

A useful trading card scanner app should show likely catalog matches for review, keep the set and card number visible, separate raw condition from existing slab grades, and connect confirmed cards to price history and a collection tracker. CardScanFox offers a separate AI pregrading tool for photo-based condition estimates. A scanner match does not authenticate a card or certify its grade.

Which trading card games does the card scanner support?

CardScanFox helps track confirmed records across supported TCG categories. Coverage varies by release, language and printing. Dragon Ball Super and Naruto coverage has not been verified. Check the current catalog before relying on identification or a price; a category guide is not proof that every card is supported.

How does CardScanFox identify a card from a photo?

Point your camera at a card, or select photos from your library. AI recognition suggests matching catalog records with details such as the set and card number. You review the match and confirm fields such as finish and language before adding it.

Can I scan more than one card at a time?

You can queue up to 10 separate camera or gallery images, then review each match before saving. This does not recognize an entire binder page from one photo. Queue capacity is separate from the lifetime allowance of 10 successful free scans; it does not reset or add free scans.

Does CardScanFox work with sports cards?

CardScanFox supports available basketball, baseball and soccer records alongside supported TCG cards. American football coverage has not been confirmed. Check the category and exact printing before relying on identification or a price.

Can I track my collection value over time?

CardScanFox can combine available published prices for your confirmed holdings and quantities, with matching history when it exists. Missing prices remain unavailable, so the total is a reference rather than a complete appraisal or guaranteed sale amount.

Does CardScanFox handle graded cards?

Yes. You can record a card as raw with its condition, or as third-party graded with the grading company and grade, covering PSA, BGS, CGC and SGC. Each is tracked and priced separately, so you can compare what the same card is worth raw versus graded.

Do I need to scan a card to look up its price?

No. You can search the card catalog by name, set or card number and open a matching record to see its current market price, price history and details. Scanning is the faster route when the card is in front of you.
