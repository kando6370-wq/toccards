Dungeon Crawler Carl trading cards: Series 1 checklist, packs and prices

2026-10-20 · Updated 2026-11-15

Dungeon Crawler Carl Series 1 is Playmates Toys' collectible trading-card release under the Borant Corporation brand. The main checklist runs from #001 to #100. Retail packs contain eight cards: seven base cards and one chase card. Renegade's Dungeon Crawler Carl Unstoppable is a separate boxed deck-building game.

Jump to the 100-card checklist, pack contents and prices, buying options or card-number guide.

Sources: Borant's official card catalog, the CardBox collector checklist, Hot Topic's pack listing and Renegade's product page; observed November 15, 2026.

Dungeon Crawler Carl Series 1: complete #001–100 checklist

The 100 numbered subjects comprise #001–080, eight Gold Member cards at #081–088, eleven Faction Wars cards at #089–099, and the Subject List at #100. Classic and Emerald versions share the numbers #001–080 and #100. CardBox's floor labels distinguish repeat characters and artwork in the table.

CardBox now has 108 catalog entries because it also records artist-sketch and signed inserts. Those extra records do not turn the main #001–100 checklist into 108 consecutive card numbers.

What is in a pack, and how much does it cost?

A Series 1 retail pack contains eight cards: seven base cards and one chase card. The release includes Emerald parallels, Gold Member gold foils, Faction Wars rainbow foils, artist sketches and Matt Dinniman signed cards. A particular chase card is random.

Where can you buy Dungeon Crawler Carl trading cards?

Borant's Where to Buy section lists Target, Barnes & Noble, GameStop, Books-A-Million and Hot Topic. Hot Topic has a direct Series 1 eight-card pack listing. Check your delivery location or local store for stock and the checkout price.

For the playable game, use Renegade's Unstoppable listing. For resale singles, request photos of the front and back so you can compare the number and finish with the checklist.

How to read the card number and variant

  1. Read the full number on the card back. Record all digits. For example, #009 Carl, #010 Carl and #089 Carl are separate checklist entries.
  2. Match the artwork and subset. #010 depicts Carl at Floor 5; #089 belongs to Faction Wars. Use both details when a seller lists only “Carl.”
  3. Record the finish separately. Classic and Emerald can share a number. #081–088 are Gold Member gold foils; #089–099 are Faction Wars rainbow foils.
  4. Save front and back photos. Include the subset and finish in a single-card search so a base-card listing does not replace the variant you own.

Example lookup: “Dungeon Crawler Carl Series 1 #017 Mordecai Emerald,” rather than “Mordecai card.” For condition and sales comparisons, see raw vs graded card values and the card price guide.

Series 1 questions

How many Dungeon Crawler Carl Series 1 cards are there?

The main numbered checklist has 100 subjects, #001–100. Classic and Emerald parallels share numbers; artist-sketch and signed inserts add further variants.

How many cards are in a Series 1 pack?

A retail pack contains eight cards: seven base cards and one chase card.

Are the trading cards the same as Unstoppable?

No. Playmates/Borant Series 1 is a collectible release. Renegade's Unstoppable is a separate solo or cooperative card-crafting deck-building game.
