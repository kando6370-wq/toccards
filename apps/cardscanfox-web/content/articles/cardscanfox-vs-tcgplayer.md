## Choose between a personal portfolio and a marketplace workflow

CardScanFox combines card identification with a personal collection and dated price references. TCGplayer combines a free mobile scanner with its TCG marketplace, pricing metrics and seller tools. Use TCGplayer if buying on its marketplace or moving scanned lists into an eligible seller account is central to your task. Use CardScanFox if you want to confirm printings, arrange owned copies in folders and keep a separate Wishlist. ## Compare the same printing and the same price metric

Suppose you have a foil copy of a Magic card. In the scanner or catalog, confirm the set and card number, then select the correct language, foil finish and condition. In TCGplayer, read the label before using the amount. Market Price reflects a group of recent sales, Listed Median describes listings, and Most Recent Sale refers to a single sale. On CardScanFox, read the source and observation date alongside the selected price. Compare two figures only when their printing and meaning fit the same question. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which app is free for ongoing TCG scanning?

TCGplayer describes its mobile app as free to use. CardScanFox can be used for browser card research and has a separate free allowance for successful scans in the iPhone app. Check which tool covers your games and how you want to save the results. [TCGplayer app FAQ](https://help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/115009506407-TCGplayer-App-FAQ) · [CardScanFox free scans and review](/app)

### Can I use CardScanFox to upload inventory to TCGplayer?

TCGplayer documents inventory import for eligible Level 4 sellers through its own mobile app. CardScanFox offers a personal Portfolio and browser card research. [TCGplayer mobile app](https://seller.tcgplayer.com/mobile-app)

### How do I compare scan accuracy?

Scan the same known cards in the same light. Record whether the first result matches the exact printing and how many corrections each tool requires. Keep that check separate from whether you prefer its price or collection workflow.
