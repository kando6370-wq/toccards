## Find your Eevee before comparing its price

“Eevee full art” is a useful starting search, but it does not identify one card. An Eevee from Brilliant Stars Trainer Gallery has a different number and set from an Eevee illustration rare in Twilight Masquerade. Promos and shiny printings add more possibilities.

Match the full number and artwork on your card to a catalog record. The links below open selected real printings, where you can check the available price and its date. This is an identification guide, not a complete list or a most-valuable ranking.

## Eevee TG11/TG30: Brilliant Stars Trainer Gallery

[Eevee TG11/TG30](/cards/264218) is cataloged in Brilliant Stars Trainer Gallery. Search with the TG prefix on both sides of the number. It belongs to the Trainer Gallery record set; searching only the main Brilliant Stars list or the number 11 can miss that distinction.

Compare the artwork and language before using the selected price. The catalog’s Holofoil finish describes this record. It does not determine the physical condition of the card in your hand.

## Eevee 188/167 vs 135/167: Twilight Masquerade

| Printing | Catalog record | Why it needs a separate comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eevee 188/167 | [Open 188/167](/cards/550232) | A distinct artwork and number above the main set denominator |
| Eevee 135/167 | [Open 135/167](/cards/550179) | A different numbered printing from the same expansion |

If you search “Eevee Twilight Masquerade price,” first choose which number is on your card. Do not transfer a price from 188/167 to 135/167. A finish or price series selected on the detail page can also change the comparison.

## Eevee SV41/SV94: Hidden Fates Shiny Vault

[Eevee SV41/SV94](/cards/197811) is cataloged in Hidden Fates: Shiny Vault. Keep the SV prefix when searching. A number such as SV41 is not interchangeable with 41 in another set, and this card should not be grouped with an Eevee GX promo merely because both show Eevee.

If your copy has a different promo number, use [Eevee catalog search](/games/pokemon?q=Eevee). Search results can contain Eevee & Snorlax, jumbo promos and deck products too. Match the product format and complete name before selecting a result.

## What affects an Eevee card’s value?

The useful comparison combines the printing with its price context. Check these fields together:

- Set, collector number, language and finish.
- Edition, stamp or promo identity where applicable.
- Raw condition, or the grading company and existing numerical grade.
- Source, currency and the date of the available observation.

A recent-looking search result does not make an older price observation current. Read the date on the detail page. If the matching record has no published price, it is still useful for identification; the missing amount is not zero.

## Should I use a raw price for a PSA 10 Eevee?

Use a reference that identifies the same grading company and grade. A raw reference is a separate comparison. There is no fixed multiplier in this guide that turns a raw amount into a PSA 10 value. See [raw vs graded card values](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) for the workflow, and [card condition terms](/blog/pokemon-card-condition-guide) for NM, LP and MP.

## Keep the card you own separate from the one you want

Use [Card AI](/download) to identify your physical card, confirm the result and record your collection. A Wishlist entry represents a card you want, while a holding represents a card you own. Keep those records separate when following collection value.
