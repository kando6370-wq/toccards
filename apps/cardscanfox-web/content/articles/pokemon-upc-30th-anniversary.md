The **Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection (UPC)** released on **November 6, 2026** in two versions: **Day** with Pikachu ex and Espeon ex, and **Night** with Pikachu ex and Umbreon ex. Each box has **29 regular booster packs, one three-card Classic Collection pack and two foil promo cards**.

Sources: [Pokémon’s US product showcase](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-product-showcase) and [TCG Ultra’s English UPC listing](https://www.tcgultra.com/products/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-ultra-premium-collection), checked November 15, 2026.

## Day vs Night: which version should you choose?

| Item | Day | Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foil promo pair | Pikachu ex + Espeon ex | Pikachu ex + Umbreon ex |
| Theme | Day-themed artwork and accessories | Night-themed artwork and accessories |
| Metal coin | Espeon | Umbreon |
| Regular boosters | 29 × 30th Celebration | 29 × 30th Celebration |
| Classic Collection booster | 1 pack with 3 cards | 1 pack with 3 cards |
| Release date | November 6, 2026 | November 6, 2026 |

Choose Day for the Espeon promo or Night for the Umbreon promo. The pack counts match. **Day and Night describe the box themes, not Japanese and English editions.** A Japanese card with the **m6a** set code belongs to the Japanese release and has its own card numbers.

## Everything inside one UPC

Each selected Day or Night collection contains:

- 1 foil Pikachu ex promo and 1 foil Espeon ex or Umbreon ex promo.
- 29 regular 30th Celebration booster packs.
- 1 Classic Collection booster pack with 3 Classic Collection cards.
- 1 playmat, 65 card sleeves and 1 deck box.
- 1 metal coin and 6 damage-counter dice.
- 1 code card for Pokémon TCG Live.

The **30-pack total is 29 + 1**, so list the Classic Collection pack separately when comparing products. The two foil promos are fixed contents; the cards pulled from the packs vary.

## Release date, price and where to buy

The US release date was **November 6, 2026**. Pokémon directs buyers to the [Pokémon Center](https://www.pokemoncenter.com/category/trading-card-game) and other Pokémon TCG retailers. Use the official [where-to-buy directory](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/where-to-buy/) to find retailers in your region.

On November 15, 2026, **TCG Ultra listed Day and Night at US$349.99 each, with both variants marked sold out**. This is that retailer’s dated asking price. Check the selected version, shipping and local availability on the linked listing before placing an order.

For a resale listing, check that it includes the sealed box, both promos and all accessories. Listings for an empty box, a promo pair or opened packs describe different items.

## How to identify the cards after opening

Keep these groups separate when sorting the contents:

| What you opened | How to identify it | Where to look it up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main expansion card | Full number such as 152/128 and set code 30C | [30th Celebration card list and prices](/blog/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-card-prices#main-card-list) |
| RGB Rare Mew | R/RGB, G/RGB or B/RGB | [RGB Mew price table](/blog/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-card-prices#rgb-mew) |
| Classic Collection card | Anniversary reprint artwork and its original-style number | [Classic Collection checklist](/blog/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-card-prices#classic-card-list) |
| Included promo | Promo identifier on the card, rather than a regular expansion number | [Pokémon card catalog](/games/pokemon) |

For example, **Mew ex 152/128** is a main-set card. **Charizard 4/102** in the Classic Collection is an anniversary reprint; its number alone also appears on the older Base Set card. Match the anniversary artwork and set as well as the number.
