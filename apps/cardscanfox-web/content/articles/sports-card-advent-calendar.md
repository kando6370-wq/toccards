## Check the product format before you order

1. Match the sport, manufacturer and year on the box photo to the listing title.
2. Look for **Advent Calendar** on the box. Compare the pack configuration against the issuer's description for that exact product. A Holiday Mega Box can use a related card design and a different pack configuration.
3. Check that all compartments are sealed. For an opened calendar, ask the seller which packs are included.
4. Compare delivered prices for the same edition. Include shipping and check the delivery estimate if the calendar is a gift.

## Identify a card from the calendar

Find the card number on the back. Match the player and card number to the checklist for the correct product year.
