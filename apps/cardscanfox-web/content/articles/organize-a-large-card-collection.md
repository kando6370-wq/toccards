## Sort in stages and record each physical location

To organize thousands of trading cards, sort by game, set and collector number first. Keep raw cards and slabs in separate storage. Record each distinct printing, condition or grade, quantity and location. Start with one box before moving the whole collection into your system.

## Give each stack a repeatable route

1. Label a box or binder and assign the stack to a set or a temporary review section.
2. Use a checklist to order the cards. Put uncertain languages, foil versions or parallels in the review section.
3. Scan or enter one manageable group. Confirm each record, then record the count and condition or slab grade.
4. Match the total from the app to the physical count before starting another group.
5. Keep wanted cards separate from owned cards. Give any future additions a temporary location until you review them.

For example, use a location such as Box A / 151 / tray 2. Two copies of the same Charizard ex printing can share a quantity if condition and location match. Another printing or a PSA slab needs a separate entry.

## Keep the app and the storage in sync

A spreadsheet can record locations and quantities if you prefer to maintain every field yourself. An app can speed up identification and pair confirmed holdings with available values. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to scan every card?

Use manual entry for known duplicates and checklists for obvious matches. Save photo scans for cards you need help identifying, and record incomplete entries in your review section.

### How can I track total collection value?

Use the quantity and reference for each exact holding. Keep missing prices visible so you can see which holdings contribute to the displayed estimate.
