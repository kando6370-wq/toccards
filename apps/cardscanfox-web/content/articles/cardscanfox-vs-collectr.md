## Compare a card, a slab and a sealed product

Take a raw Pokémon card, a graded copy of the same printing and a sealed product from the same set. Use the full collector number, language and finish to confirm each single. Keep the raw condition and the slab label separate, then check the date and source for each price. Look up the sealed product by its own name and contents. This shows whether you need a workflow for exact copies, a combined portfolio spanning singles and sealed products, or both.

If the two tools give different amounts, check whether both refer to the same printing, grade, time period and currency. A recent sold listing, a published market figure and an active asking price answer different questions.
