Takumi Akabane has illustration credits on Pokémon Trading Card Game cards, including several Special Energy printings. This guide lists examples and shows how to identify an exact printing before you compare prices.

## Which Pokémon cards credit Takumi Akabane?

Start with these Japanese examples from [The Art of Pokémon’s illustrator catalog](https://www.artofpkm.com/illustrators/takumi-akabane). Use the set and number together when you look up a card.

| Card | Set or release | Collector number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Darkness Energy | Beat of the Frontier | 096/100 |
| Metal Energy | Beat of the Frontier | 097/100 |
| Rainbow Energy | Galactic’s Conquest | 093/096 |
| Rainbow Energy | DPt-P Promotional cards | 021/DPt-P |

Rainbow Energy appears twice in this sample. The expansion card and the Japanese promo have different collector numbers. Each printing needs its own price comparison.

## How do I identify and value my card?

1. Read the illustrator credit and copy the full collector number.
2. Check the set, language, finish and any promotion marks against the reference image.
3. Search the [Pokémon card catalog](/games/pokemon) or use the [Pokémon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner) to find candidate records. Confirm the printing before choosing a result.
4. Compare dated prices for the exact card in comparable raw condition or the same certified grade. Use the [card condition guide](/blog/pokemon-card-condition-guide) to check common raw condition terms.

For a signed card, check the signature certification and provenance separately. The illustration credit identifies the artist on the printing. Signature authentication requires its own evidence.
