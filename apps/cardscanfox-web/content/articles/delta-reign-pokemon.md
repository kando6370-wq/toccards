## How do the Legendary Stadium halves work?

A **Legendary Stadium combines two Stadium cards** to form extended artwork and a gameplay feature. Pokémon’s announcement says that a booster containing a Legendary Stadium includes both required halves. This is about the contents of a qualifying pack, not a promise of a Stadium in every pack. When checking a listing, confirm whether the seller is offering a pair or one half.

Choose a product for the experience you want. Packs provide unopened cards. An Elite Trainer Box adds its stated promo and accessories. A single-card listing lets you select the identified printing rather than opening packs to find it. Compare the sale unit, edition and contents before comparing two totals.

## Are the launch retailer promos inside the ETB?

The US launch gift was a separate offer for a qualifying **Pokémon TCG purchase of $15 or more**. It used the **“What’s Your Favorite?” stamp**: Groudon at GameStop, Giratina at Best Buy, and Kyogre at Hot Topic, Five Below, Barnes & Noble and Macy’s. The offer began November 6 while supplies lasted, with participating-retailer terms. [See the US announcement](https://press.pokemon.com/en/MEDIA-ALERT-Legendary-Pokemon-Mega-Rayquaza-ex-Descends-into-Battle-in).

That stamp and the Pokémon Center logo identify different promotions. An ETB’s included Kyogre, a Center-logo Kyogre and a retailer gift should be checked separately. Ask for a clear image of the stamp and confirm whether a listed bonus is included with the purchase. A sealed box’s contents do not establish a store’s remaining gift supply.

## How do you confirm the card number and check its value?

1. Read the full collector number, including both parts of a slash number.
2. Match the expansion, artwork and language.
3. Match the finish, such as the specific foil treatment, before choosing a price series.
4. Read the source, currency and observation date attached to that reference.
5. Compare the same condition and object type: a loose card, an existing graded copy and a sealed box are different purchases.

The [Pokémon catalog](/games/pokemon) and [card search](/cards?game=pokemon&q=Delta%20Reign) are entry points for supported records. CardScanFox’s Delta Reign set currently has no public single-card entries, so use the official material to establish the printing first. A missing catalog row is not a zero value. An older Rayquaza amount does not fill that gap.

For an available record, compare the same price series over time. A large percentage rise can start from a low amount, and a raw reference does not value a graded slab. The [raw-versus-graded guide](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) explains that distinction. Record the printing and source with your comparison so you can check like-for-like amounts later.
