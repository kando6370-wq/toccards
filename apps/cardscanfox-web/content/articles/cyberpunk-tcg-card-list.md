## Choose the set that matches your card

| Card came from | Choose this filter in the official database |
| --- | --- |
| English Welcome to Night City release | Welcome to Night City — Retail |
| Early Welcome to Night City release | Welcome to Night City — Beta |
| French retail booster | Welcome to Night City — Retail — FR |
| Embracing Power or The Heist deck | The matching starter deck name and edition |
| Box topper, event or judge distribution | The matching topper or promo set |

Search the full card name, then open the entry and match the set, art, language and complete collector number. Keep suffixes such as the `a` in `005a`. For example, the [official V — Streetkid record](https://cyberpunktcg.com/cards/v-streetkid?printing=3fc63c58-5954-4744-a5af-047bfc5cb159) shows the English retail printing. Use that exact printing when you compare marketplace listings.

## Check a sealed starter deck against its deck list

WeirdCo lists three Legends and 40 main-deck cards per starter deck. **Embracing Power** includes the Legends Goro Takemura — Hands Unclean, Yorinobu Arasaka — Embracing Destruction, and Saburo Arasaka — Stubborn Patriarch. **The Heist** includes V — Corporate Exile, Viktor Vektor — Sit Down and Relax, and Jackie Welles — Pour One Out For Me. Use the [official starter deck lists](https://cyberpunktcg.com/blog/wnc-starter-decks) to check every card and quantity.
