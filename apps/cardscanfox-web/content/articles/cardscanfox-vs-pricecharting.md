CardScanFox suits collectors who want to confirm the exact printing, check a dated reference and keep records of their physical cards. PriceCharting suits collectors who want to inspect sold-listing evidence, compare grade-specific values and track several types of collectibles. Both can help identify a card from a photo. ## Compare one raw card and one grade at a time

For a Pokémon Charizard you already own, write down the set, full collector number, language and finish. Look up the exact card in both tools. If it is raw, select an available condition-specific series in CardScanFox and read how PriceCharting labels its ungraded figure. If the card is already graded, compare rows for the same grading company and grade. Open matching sold records where available and check whether they include a different printing, bundle or shipping cost. [CardScanFox price checker](/card-price-checker) · [PriceCharting price methodology](https://www.pricecharting.com/page/methodology)

For a binder page, the unit of work differs. PriceCharting accepts one image containing multiple cards through its Multi Scan tool. CardScanFox accepts multiple individual photos into a mobile review queue. Try the workflow you would use before committing a full binder. ## Frequently asked questions

### Why can the two tools show different prices?

They can show different references, date ranges or condition groups. Compare the data source, observation dates and the exact printing first. A PriceCharting guide value aggregates transactions under its own method. CardScanFox displays the price and date for a selected available series. [CardScanFox methodology](/data-methodology) · [PriceCharting methodology](https://www.pricecharting.com/page/methodology)

### Which is better for a collection that also has games or comics?

PriceCharting includes games and comics in its collection tracker. CardScanFox focuses on trading-card identification and card holdings, including raw and existing graded copies. [CardScanFox app guide](/app) · [PriceCharting collection tracker](https://www.pricecharting.com/page/collection-tracker)

### Does a price lookup assess my card's grade?

Price lookups use the grade or condition you select. An existing slab label provides the grade for comparison. CardScanFox also offers a separate photo pregrading workflow for visible condition. [CardScanFox AI pregrading](/ai-card-grading)
