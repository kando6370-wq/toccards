## Pick the app for the cards you own and what comes after the scan

Collectr fits a TCG collector who wants portfolios for cards and sealed products, price history and trade analysis. Ludex fits a sports or supported TCG collector who wants to scan cards, review recent sales and create eBay listings on an eligible plan. Check exact game support before choosing. ## Two collections lead to different choices

For Pokémon singles, sealed boxes and One Piece cards, check whether you can track all three in a Collectr portfolio and inspect a sample price history before purchasing PRO. If you collect baseball and basketball parallels and plan to sell on eBay, try Ludex with a base card and a parallel from the same year and set. Check the listing handoff and the plan's publishing limit before subscribing.

If your collection mixes sports with several TCGs, try each product on the same small sample before entering a whole collection. Record the exact match, whether the price comes from a transaction or an estimate, and the steps needed to fix a wrong variant.

### Is Ludex better for One Piece?

Collectr lists One Piece on its scanner page. Ludex's mobile help page says One Piece is unsupported, even though Ludex's separate marketplace search displays a One Piece category. Check scanner coverage independently of marketplace search categories.
