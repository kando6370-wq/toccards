## Which card does “Bubble Mew” mean?

“Bubble Mew” is a collector nickname used for Mew ex artwork. For the English card discussed here, match [Mew ex 232/091 from Paldean Fates](/cards/534919). The catalog names the card Mew ex and identifies its full number and set. Do not use the nickname alone to select a price.

Other languages and printings require their own records. A similar image or seller title is a clue to investigate, not confirmation that the English reference applies.

Both belong to Paldean Fates, but their shared name does not make them one price category. Keep the complete denominator when searching. A reference for 232/091 should not be assigned to 216/091.

## Mew ex in Scarlet & Violet—151

The following records belong to a different expansion from the Paldean Fates examples:

- [Mew ex 151/165](/cards/516566).
- [Mew ex 193/165](/cards/517027).
- [Mew ex 205/165](/cards/517051).

Choose the number printed on your card. “Mew ex 151” can refer to the expansion name or the 151/165 collector number, so check both fields. It does not identify Bubble Mew 232/091.

## Mew ex 152/128 is a different 30th Celebration printing

[Mew ex 152/128](/cards/717605) belongs to **ME: 30th Celebration**. It is not the Scarlet & Violet—151 card numbered 151/165, and it is not the English Bubble Mew 232/091 from Paldean Fates.

Keep both parts of the collector number. “Mew 152” alone does not establish the set, language or finish. Open the [30th Celebration guide](/blog/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-card-prices) for the release context, then read the selected price series and observation date on the exact record.

## Two 205/165 products: regular card or metal card?

The catalog also contains a separate [Mew ex 205/165 metal-card record](/cards/519481). It shares a collector number with the regular [205/165 card](/cards/517051), but the material and product identity differ.

Check which product you physically own before using either reference. The printed number alone cannot resolve this case. Keep a metal-card reference separate from a standard trading-card comparison, just as you would separate an oversized product from a standard-size card.

## Is Mew V or Mew GG10 the same card?

No. [Mew V 113/264 from Fusion Strike](/cards/253274) is a different named card and numbered printing. The existing [Mew GG10/GG70 guide](/blog/mew-gg10-card-value) covers the Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery card. Neither should use the Paldean Fates Mew ex reference.

Mewtwo is also a different Pokémon. Use the [Mewtwo EX, GX and ex guide](/blog/mewtwo-card-values) if that is the name on your copy.

## Check the price context after identification

Open the exact record for its available amount, source and observation date. Confirm language, finish, raw condition or existing grading-company/grade context before comparing it with your copy. The guide does not write a fixed Bubble Mew amount into the article or infer a current price spike from an old search result.

Use the [raw vs graded value guide](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) for slab comparisons. If a matching reference is missing, do not fill the gap with another Mew printing's amount.
