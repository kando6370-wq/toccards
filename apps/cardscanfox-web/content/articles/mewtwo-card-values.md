## Which Mewtwo Pokémon card is yours?

Mewtwo EX, Mewtwo GX and Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex are different card names. Even within one set, several numbered Mewtwo cards can exist. Match the exact name and full collector number before using a price.

The examples below link to real catalog records. Open a record to inspect its artwork, language, finish and available dated price reference. This selection helps identify a card; it does not rank every Mewtwo by value.

## Mewtwo EX: BREAKthrough 61/162 vs 62/162

| Card | Expansion | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mewtwo EX 61/162](/cards/107180) | XY — BREAKthrough | Match the number and artwork |
| [Mewtwo EX 62/162](/cards/107181) | XY — BREAKthrough | Compare this separate printing with your copy |

Both names contain Mewtwo EX and both records belong to BREAKthrough. The number is therefore essential. A full-art or secret printing from the expansion also needs its own record; the set name alone is not enough.

Keep standard-size cards separate from jumbo promos. A jumbo card can share a familiar name or number but is a different product. Search results are candidates, not proof that the physical format matches.

## Mewtwo GX: Shining Legends 39/73 vs 78/73

[Mewtwo GX 39/73](/cards/146695) and [Mewtwo GX 78/73](/cards/146698) are separate Shining Legends records. The catalog labels the second as Secret Shining. Compare its artwork with your card instead of applying its price to every Mewtwo GX.

The number above the denominator helps distinguish 78/73 from 39/73. It does not, by itself, authenticate the copy or establish a professional grade. Inspect the language and printing details as well.

## Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex: 081/182 vs 231/182

Destined Rivals contains several Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex printings. Start with these two distinct records:

- [Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex 081/182](/cards/632891).
- [Team Rocket’s Mewtwo ex 231/182](/cards/633031).

The shared name does not make their artwork or price interchangeable. If your copy is numbered 213/182, 240/182 or something else, [search the Mewtwo catalog](/games/pokemon?q=Mewtwo) and select that exact record. Do not round a collector number to the nearest example in this guide.

## How much is my Mewtwo card worth?

Open the matching printing and read the price context before using the amount. The detail page identifies the available source, metric, condition or existing grade, language, finish and observation date. A published reference is a comparison point, not an offer to buy your card.

For a raw card, check its physical condition. For a slab, compare the named grading company and actual grade on the holder. A raw Near Mint reference should not be presented as a PSA 9 or PSA 10 amount. Read the [raw vs graded guide](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) and [condition guide](/blog/pokemon-card-condition-guide) before comparing those categories.

If the relevant price or history is missing, the site leaves it missing. It does not use another Mewtwo’s price to fill the gap.
