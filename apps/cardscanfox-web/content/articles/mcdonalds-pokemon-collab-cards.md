## McDonald’s Pokémon Happy Meal cards

The 2026 McDonald’s Pokémon collab is a limited Happy Meal promotion with Pokémon toys and Pokémon TCG cards. The official Canada announcement says each meal includes two cards, with 15 cards and 15 toys in the collection.

Dates, toy rules and availability vary by region and while supplies last. This page uses a region table so a Canada date does not become a global launch date.
