## Find history for one matching price series

Find trading card price history by starting with the set, full collector number, language and variant. Then select the same raw condition or grading company and grade across the dates you compare. Keep the source, currency and metric consistent. A change of source or condition can change the chart even when the card’s market has not moved.

| When you need | Look for | Avoid combining |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A daily or weekly trend | Dated observations from the same reference series | Raw and slabbed values on one line |
| Evidence for a sale | Comparable completed transactions | Unsold asking prices, bundles and different grades |
| A longer price history | A provider that covers the exact version and period | Gaps treated as days with zero change |

## Read the source and dates

TCGplayer documents a Market Price based on recent transactions on its marketplace. PriceCharting describes an aggregate derived from sales records. eBay Product Research provides completed sale data to eligible sellers. These measures answer different questions. For a sale comparison, note the matching item's price, shipping and date.

For example, if the first observation is for a raw English card and the second is for a PSA 10 copy, they cannot measure the same movement. If your selected series has three observations in the month, chart those three with their dates and keep the other days blank.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does an old price still apply today?

Treat it as an observation on its date. If the card trades infrequently, report how many examples you found and when they occurred.

### Can I use the amount on an active listing?

An active listing shows a seller’s asking price. Compare completed sales for evidence of what buyers paid.
