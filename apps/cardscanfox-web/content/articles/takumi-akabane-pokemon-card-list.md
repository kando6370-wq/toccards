## Takumi Akabane is an artist and early TCG figure

Takumi Akabane is an early Pokémon Trading Card Game figure whose name appears in artist and historical card records. This page is an artist checklist, not a new expansion page.

Confirm the printed illustrator credit and language before you add a card to a personal checklist. An artist database can collect related records, but each row still needs its own card identity.

## Checklist fields

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Card name | Identifies the printed Pokémon or Trainer |
| Language | Keeps English and Japanese records separate |
| Set | Separates reprints and products |
| Collector number | Confirms the exact card record |
| Year | Helps place early and later records |
| Printed illustrator | Confirms the credit shown on the card |

Do not merge a signed card, a game-design credit or a card featuring a Pokémon character into a printed illustrator credit without source evidence.

## A checklist is an identity aid

An artist checklist helps identify a card record. It does not authenticate a physical card, prove a signature or set a market value. Use the card record and its dated price context only after you confirm the exact printing.
