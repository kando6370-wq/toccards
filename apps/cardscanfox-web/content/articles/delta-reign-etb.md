## Which Delta Reign ETB promo or stamp do you want?

There are three different promotion types to recognize. The regular ETB includes its Kyogre card. The Center box includes a regular Kyogre promo plus a Kyogre promo carrying the Pokémon Center logo. A seller describing a “stamped promo” should show which stamp is on the card.

The launch also had a separate US retailer gift offer for eligible Pokémon TCG purchases of **$15 or more**. Those cards carried a **“What’s Your Favorite?” stamp**. The official announcement named Groudon at GameStop, Giratina at Best Buy, and Kyogre at several other participating retailers. These gifts were available from November 6 while supplies lasted. A retailer gift is separate from the contents sealed inside an ETB. Check the [official US promotion details](https://press.pokemon.com/en/MEDIA-ALERT-Legendary-Pokemon-Mega-Rayquaza-ex-Descends-into-Battle-in) before planning a purchase around a bonus card.

## How should you compare a Delta Reign ETB case?

A single ETB and an ETB case are different sale units. The same page showed **$793.07 Market Price**, **$716.95 Most Recent Sale** and **$825 Listed Median**; those are different price measures, not interchangeable checkout offers.

For that advertised offer and its stated quantity:

**($711.96 + $5 shipping) ÷ 10 ETBs = about $71.70 per ETB before tax.**

## Has Delta Reign released, or is this a preorder?
