The Pokémon Company announced **Mega Evolution—Flowing Fists** for retail release on **February 6, 2027**. Prerelease events start **January 23, 2027**, and the set arrives in Pokémon TCG Live on **February 4, 2027**. The announcement features **Mega Lucario Z, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Absol Z and Mega Glimmora**. The full English collector-number checklist remains unverified for this guide.
