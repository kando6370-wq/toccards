## “151” identifies the expansion, not one Charmander

The search “Charmander 151” usually needs one more detail: the collector number. Our English Scarlet & Violet—151 catalog contains a Charmander numbered 004/165 and a separate printing numbered 168/165. They have different records and need separate price comparisons.

Read the number on your physical card before selecting a result. The leading zero in 004/165 is formatting; the distinction between 004/165 and 168/165 is the important one. Match the artwork and language too.

## Is a reverse-holo Charmander the same comparison?

Collector number and finish answer different questions. A number identifies a numbered printing, while the price series can also distinguish its finish. Check the selected series on the detail page against your copy. Do not use a normal or reverse-holo reference for a different illustration-rare card.

Card artwork is useful for matching a printing. It does not establish physical condition. Scratches, edge wear or other defects on your copy still matter when comparing a raw reference. Use the [NM, LP and MP condition guide](/blog/pokemon-card-condition-guide) if those terms are unfamiliar.

## What if my card is Japanese or already graded?

Use a record and reference that identify the same language. The English examples above do not price a Japanese copy simply because both show Charmander. Search the exact number and compare the language and set name in the result.

For an existing slab, use the grading company and grade on the holder. A raw Near Mint observation is not a PSA 9 or PSA 10 amount. Read [raw vs graded card values](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) before comparing them. A missing grade or condition series remains unavailable.

## Is this the same search as Charizard 151?

Charmander and Charizard are different Pokémon and different catalog records. The shared expansion does not make their values interchangeable. The existing [Charizard 151 guide](/blog/charizard-151-card-value) compares Charizard ex 006/165, 183/165 and 199/165; use it if one of those numbers matches your card.
