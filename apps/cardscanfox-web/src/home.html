All questions __ICON_ARROW_RIGHT__
What kinds of cards can I look up? __ICON_CHEVRON_DOWN__

Browse supported TCG and sports-card records. Catalog coverage and published prices vary by game, printing and market data. This preview contains 30 selected public records.

Do I need to scan a card to find a price? __ICON_CHEVRON_DOWN__

No. Search by name, set or card number. Confirm the printing and the copy’s condition or existing grade before using an available price reference.

Does the app authenticate cards or predict grades? __ICON_CHEVRON_DOWN__

No. A photo suggests catalog candidates for your review. CardScanFox records raw condition or the grade already printed on an existing slab.

Why can a card have no price? __ICON_CHEVRON_DOWN__

A catalog record can exist without a matching published price. Missing coverage does not mean zero value. A different printing’s amount is not substituted.

What does saving a card on this website do? __ICON_CHEVRON_DOWN__

Preview saves stay on this browser and device. Portfolio, folders and Wishlist are managed in the app.
