## Description

Recognize supported Pokémon, TCG, and sports-card images while you browse. TCG Card Recognizer works on regular webpages; it does not run on Chrome internal pages or the Chrome Web Store.

Hover over a card image that is at least 180 × 220 pixels and select Recognize. Choose TCG or Sports Card, adjust the crop, and confirm. The extension extracts an image vector on your device, then shows likely card matches and available details in a panel on the same page.

Review candidate cards, match confidence, card details, and available reference prices or price changes. Results depend on image quality and catalog coverage. A match is a suggestion, not a guarantee of the exact printing. Check the set and card number before using a result. Reference prices are not guaranteed transaction values.

The source image is processed on your device. The recognition service receives the image vector and selected card type, not the original image. Online matching requires an internet connection.

TCG Card Recognizer is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, or other trading card publishers. Pokémon and related names are trademarks of their respective owners.
