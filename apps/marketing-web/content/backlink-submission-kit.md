## 产品目录英文文案

### Name

Card AI

### Tagline

Scan, confirm, and track your physical card collection

### Short description

Card AI helps physical trading card collectors find likely card matches from a photo or catalog search, confirm the printing, and keep the cards they own in a collection with available published prices and price history.

### Full description

Card AI is a mobile scanner and collection tracker for physical trading cards. Start with a camera photo, a gallery image, or a catalog search by name, set, or card number. Review the suggested record and confirm the printing, finish, and language before saving it.

Keep raw copies and existing graded slabs as distinct holdings. Add cards you own to a portfolio, keep cards you want on a wishlist, and review the selected record's available published prices and price history.

A scan suggests a match for review. It does not authenticate a card or guarantee an exact printing. Prices depend on the selected record and market coverage, and they are reference values rather than guaranteed sale amounts. Card AI does not currently predict a raw card's grade from a photo.

### Feature bullets

- Find likely catalog matches from card photos.
- Search by card name, set, or card number.
- Confirm printing, finish, and language before saving.
- Keep raw conditions and existing slab grades distinct.
- Organize owned cards and maintain a separate wishlist.
- Review available published prices and price history.
