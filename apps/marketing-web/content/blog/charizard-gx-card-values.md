Charizard GX is not one printing. The name appears on Hidden Fates, Shiny Vault, promotional and Burning Shadows records. Even a 9/68 search can lead to both a regular Hidden Fates card and a Battle Academy stamped copy. Confirm the product as well as the number before checking what your card is worth.

## Same number, different product

Look for the Battle Academy stamp when comparing the two 9/68 records. Leading zeroes in a catalog number do not establish a different physical card on their own; the product and stamp are the important distinction here.

The catalog also has jumbo promo cards with familiar SM numbers. Those oversized items are excluded. Code cards and sealed collections are not physical Charizard GX singles and are excluded as well. A search result that contains the words Charizard GX does not automatically belong in your card's valuation.
