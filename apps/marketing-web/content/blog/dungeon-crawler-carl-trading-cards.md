Looking for Dungeon Crawler Carl trading cards? Playmates announced a collectible-card release, and retailers list Series 1 packs. The separate Unstoppable deck-building game is for play. Check which product you mean before looking for a card list, a seller or a price.

## Which Dungeon Crawler Carl cards are being sold?

- **Collectible trading cards.** [Playmates Toys announced a Dungeon Crawler Carl trading-card rollout](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/playmates-toys_playmates-toys-inc-in-partnership-with-books-a-million-activity-7462587604455231488--M6O) with Books-A-Million. [Hot Topic lists a Series 1 trading-card pack](https://www.hottopic.com/product/dungeon-crawler-carl-series-1-trading-card-pack/37061648.html). Check the issuer and packaging before assuming that cards from different sellers belong to the same release.
- **Unstoppable game cards.** [Renegade Game Studios describes Unstoppable](https://dungeoncrawlercarl.renegadegamestudios.com/dungeon-crawler-carl-unstoppable/) as a card-crafting deck-building game. Its playable cards are not a checklist for the collectible trading-card release.

## Is there a Dungeon Crawler Carl Series 1 card list?

As of October 30, 2026, we have not verified a card-by-card checklist from the Series 1 issuer. [CardBox shows a third-party Series One checklist](https://cardbox.club/price-guide/dungeon-crawler-carl/dungeon-crawler-carl-series-one-9c845a) with 100 cards documented. Use it to look up card names and numbers, but do not treat it as an issuer checklist or a verified price guide. Its catalog showed no cards with a value when checked.

For a card you have opened, look for the issuer, release name, printed number, character, artwork and any foil or limited marking. A character name alone cannot confirm the printing.

## Where can you buy Dungeon Crawler Carl trading cards?

[Hot Topic's Series 1 pack listing](https://www.hottopic.com/product/dungeon-crawler-carl-series-1-trading-card-pack/37061648.html) was out of stock when checked on October 30, 2026. Check its current availability and shipping region before ordering. Playmates also announced a rollout with Books-A-Million; that announcement does not confirm current stock at a particular store. Card AI does not sell these packs.

## Check the card before checking value

Keep a loose single separate from a sealed pack. Compare the card's visible details with the release you identified before using a price.

Card AI suggests catalog matches for supported physical cards. A Dungeon Crawler Carl card is not guaranteed to have a matching record or price. If a suggested result does not match the release and visible variant, do not save another printing as a substitute. Card AI does not authenticate a card, predict a grade or guarantee a sale price.
