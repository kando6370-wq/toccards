Dungeon Crawler Carl has more than one kind of physical card. A collectible trading card, a small character-card set and a card from the Unstoppable deck-building game are different products. **Start with the issuer and release on the packaging or card** before searching for a match or comparing prices.

## Which Dungeon Crawler Carl cards are being discussed?

- **World Earth trading cards.** [Playmates Toys announced a Dungeon Crawler Carl trading-card rollout](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/playmates-toys_playmates-toys-inc-in-partnership-with-books-a-million-activity-7462587604455231488--M6O) with Books-A-Million. Collectors discuss pack pulls, characters and special versions from this collectible line.
- **Character collector cards.** [ShelfCare Bindery's five-card set](https://shelfcarebindery.com/products/dungeon-crawler-carl-collectors-cards-duarduart-x-shelfcare-bindery) is a separate product. A card from that set should not be recorded as a Playmates card because the character name happens to match.
- **Unstoppable game cards.** [Renegade Game Studios describes Unstoppable](https://dungeoncrawlercarl.renegadegamestudios.com/dungeon-crawler-carl-unstoppable/) as a standalone card-crafting deck-building game. Its playable cards are not the same release as the collectible trading cards.

The [collector discussion about recent pack pulls](https://www.reddit.com/r/DungeonCrawlerCarl/comments/1x8ngrb/pulls_from_1_pack_of_dcc_trading_cards/) shows why release, artwork and variant matter to readers. A post or seller listing is useful context, but it is not an official checklist or a verified sale price.

## Check the card before checking value

Look for the release name, issuer, card number if printed, character, artwork and any foil or limited marking. Keep a loose single separate from a sealed pack. Two cards showing Carl or Princess Donut can come from different releases, so a character-only match is not enough.

Card AI supports scanning cataloged Dungeon Crawler Carl collectible cards. Photograph the card, review the suggested catalog match and confirm the release and visible variant before saving it. Availability of a matching record, image and market price depends on the specific card. If the suggested record does not match, do not save it as another printing. Card AI does not authenticate a card, predict a grade or guarantee a sale price.
