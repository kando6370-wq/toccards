A Pokemon name is a starting point for a price check. It is not a complete card identity. Choose a character below, then match the set, full collector number, language and finish to the card you hold. These guides compare selected English catalog records. They are not a complete Pokemon card database or a ranking of every valuable card.

## Check a number within its set

Keep both sides of a collector number. A result for 199/165 is not identified by 199 alone. Gallery prefixes such as GG and SV also matter. If the same number appears in different products, check the artwork, stamp and product identity as well.

For a numbered set, browse the searchable [Ascended Heroes card list](/blog/ascended-heroes-card-list), [Chaos Rising card list](/blog/chaos-rising-card-list) or [Phantasmal Flames card list](/blog/phantasmal-flames-card-list). These lists retain official English numbers and show separately dated catalog price samples. A set checklist is different from the cards you personally own.

## Read the price date before using a value

Each guide states which records it compares and when their price samples were observed. The sample does not identify your card's raw condition or an existing slab grade unless stated. A published reference is not an offer, an individual completed sale or a guaranteed resale value. Missing prices must remain missing.

Older records need extra care. In the November 5 catalog lookup, Shining Charizard 107/105 from Neo Destiny appeared under a 1st Edition Holofoil record, but its price date was May 18, 2026. That old observation cannot establish a current Shining Charizard value, and the record cannot price an unlimited or ungraded copy by itself. Shining Fates Charizard VMAX SV107/SV122 is a different card despite the similar search words.

## Move from a reference to your own card

Use the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner) or search by name, set and number in the Card AI app. Review the candidate, confirm the printing, finish and language, then choose the raw condition or grade already printed on a slab. Open the [card price checker](/card-price-checker) for the selected record's available price and history. Save cards you own in your [collection tracker](/card-collection-tracker).

The website guides provide public references. Scanning, personal holdings and collection values are App workflows. A guide entry does not guarantee a scan match, a price for every condition, or card authenticity.
