## Why most-valuable lists differ

A list can rank raw references, one grading company and grade, record auctions, or only a selected set. It may also combine languages or promos. Check what the list includes, which date it uses, and whether it quotes asking prices or sales. A rare card's headline result does not value every card with the same character.

Confirm the full collector number and artwork, language and finish. Use the [Pokemon set reference guide](/blog/pokemon-tcg-set-card-lists) to separate Surging Sparks from another expansion. The [30th Celebration guide](/blog/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-card-prices) covers a different anniversary set, while the [Ascended Heroes checklist](/blog/ascended-heroes-card-list) has its own Pikachu entries.
