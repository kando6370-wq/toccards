## Why the set and finish matter

Do not apply the 076/064 reference to a 041/064 copy just because both names say Cufant. Match the artwork and printed number. Keep the SV prefix when searching for the Shiny Vault card; dropping it can point to a different numbered entry.

The catalog finish shown in this table belongs to that record. If your physical copy has a different treatment or language, locate its own record rather than assuming the same sample applies. An unavailable finish price remains unavailable.
