Find an English Chaos Rising card by name or collector number below. The numbered list follows the [official Pokemon Chaos Rising checklist](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/galleries/chaos-rising/). Published USD examples come from Card AI's catalog on November 5, 2026, with a separate date for each price.

## Confirm CRI and the printed number

Card AI lists this set as ME04: Chaos Rising with the code CRI. Check the name, artwork, language and full collector number on the physical card. For example, Xerneas 042/086 and Xerneas 091/086 are separate printings. A character name alone does not select the price for your copy.

## Use the list as a reference, then confirm your copy

Use the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner) when you have the card in hand, or use catalog search in the Card AI app by name, set or number. Review the candidate and confirm the finish and language. Enter a raw condition or the grade already printed on a slab before inspecting any available prices for that holding.

The table is a dated reference and does not save a collection or scan a photo in your browser. A missing price is not zero. Use the [card price checker guide](/card-price-checker) to understand the difference between a published reference, an asking price and a comparable sale.

## Checklist, chase card and sealed-product questions

A chase-card list is a selection, while the numbered checklist identifies the expansion's cards. The price samples here are not a prediction of which cards to buy or a guarantee that the most expensive sample will sell for that amount. A booster pack, box or code card is a different object from a numbered single card and is not priced by this table.
