[WatsonTCG](https://watsontcg.com/) sells cards and sealed products from several games. It is a store, not a separate TCG. **Card AI scans a supported card by its game and printing, regardless of which shop sold it.** For a WatsonTCG purchase, the first question is what is inside the package or which single card you own.

## Match the card to its game

WatsonTCG lists products from Pokemon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Disney Lorcana and other lines. Card AI has catalog and scan workflows for those four card games. It also supports other listed games, but the store name alone does not identify a card or prove every item in the shop is covered.

- For a **Pokemon single**, confirm the expansion, collector number, finish and language. Use the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner).
- For a **Magic single**, check the set symbol, collector number and foil or alternate treatment. Use the [MTG card scanner](/mtg-card-scanner).
- For a **One Piece or Lorcana single**, confirm the artwork and variant against the suggested record. See the [One Piece scanner](/one-piece-card-scanner) or [Lorcana scanner](/lorcana-card-scanner).

The shop also lists products such as Riftbound and sealed booster boxes. This guide does not claim Card AI can identify every game, sealed product or item in WatsonTCG's changing inventory. Open the pack first if your task is to catalog its individual cards.

## Scan after opening or buying a single

Photograph one card or add up to 10 camera or gallery items to the current scan queue. Review each suggested match before saving. Confirm the game, set, number, language, finish and raw condition or existing slab grade. A shop's asking price is not the same as the published market price for your exact copy, and neither amount guarantees a sale.

Card AI is an independent collection and price-checking app. This guide does not imply a partnership with WatsonTCG or access to its order records.
