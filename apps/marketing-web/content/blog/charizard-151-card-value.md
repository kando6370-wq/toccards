Charizard 151 is not a unique card identifier. The English Scarlet & Violet—151 expansion includes several Charizard ex printings. Start with the [official 151 checklist](https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/checklist/mew_web_cardlist_en.pdf) and the [official Charizard ex 199/165 record](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/sv3pt5/199/) before comparing values.

## Artwork, language and card state

Read the full printed number, compare the artwork, and confirm the language. A number above the printed denominator still belongs to a specific printing; the shared Charizard ex name does not remove that distinction.

For a raw copy, use the condition you can observe. For an existing slab, use the company and grade on its label. Card AI records those states separately; it does not predict an official grade from a photo or authenticate a copy.
