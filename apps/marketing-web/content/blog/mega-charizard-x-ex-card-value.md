Mega Charizard X ex appears at multiple numbers in the English Phantasmal Flames expansion. Use the [official gallery and checklist](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/galleries/phantasmal-flames/) to identify the artwork and number before choosing a price. This guide covers PFL single-card records, not an Ultra-Premium Collection or sealed packs.

The shared name does not make these prices interchangeable. Confirm the printed number and artwork, then the finish and language. These samples do not specify raw condition or slab grade, so none of these amounts is a promise for a particular physical copy.

## Use a single-card reference for a single card

A Mega Charizard X ex Ultra-Premium Collection search asks about a packaged product. Its contents and unopened value are different from the four singles above. A card bought in a collection box still needs its own set and number after it is removed from the packaging.

The [Phantasmal Flames card list](/blog/phantasmal-flames-card-list) shows every numbered English checklist entry and separately dated catalog samples. Code cards and unnumbered extras remain outside that numbered list.

## Confirm a scan before checking its value

Use a photo or text search in the Card AI app. Review the likely record in the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner), then select the raw condition or grade already shown on a slab. The scan suggests a candidate; it does not authenticate the card or forecast its grade.

The [card price checker](/card-price-checker) explains how to inspect available history and distinguish a published reference from a marketplace listing. If your printing or grade has no value, do not borrow the price from another row. To follow your confirmed copy over time, add it to the [collection tracker](/card-collection-tracker).
