## What to confirm on the physical card

Read the name Mew and the complete GG10/GG70 number. Match the artwork, English language and finish to the selected record. A different language, gallery number or Mew printing needs its own reference. Keep a photo or seller title as a clue until those details agree.

If your card is in a slab, use the grading company and grade printed on its label. A raw card does not acquire a PSA 10 value because the artwork looks clean in a photo. Review [raw versus graded card values](/blog/raw-vs-graded-card-values) before comparing the two markets.
