## Why 111/203 needs more than a number

The catalog also has a Jumbo Cards Rayquaza VMAX 111/203 record and a Prize Pack Series Cards record with that number. They are excluded from the table above. A larger card or a stamped product record must not borrow the regular Evolving Skies record's price. Confirm the product, size, artwork and any stamp before selecting a candidate.

The [official Evolving Skies 218/203 page](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/swsh7/218/) provides an artwork and number reference. Use the printed number and artwork together rather than relying only on a seller's descriptive label. Japanese records are also separate from the English samples shown here.
