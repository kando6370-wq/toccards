## One card number can have several finishes

Card AI identifies the expansion as ME: Ascended Heroes, code ASC. Its catalog can hold several records for one printed number, including different patterns or finishes. Those records are not extra checklist numbers. Keep the pattern visible when you compare prices, and confirm the artwork and language before choosing a holding.

The table retains all official numbered entries even when the snapshot has no matching record. Team Rocket's Energy 217/217 is one such entry in this snapshot. It remains in the checklist with no substitute price.

## How to compare an Ascended Heroes card

Start with the collector number, then compare the card's name and image with the [official gallery](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/galleries/ascended-heroes/). Inspect the foil or pattern on the copy you own. A plain finish and an Energy Symbol Pattern must not silently share a reference price.

In the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner), a photo produces candidates for review. Confirm the record, language and finish, then choose a raw condition or enter the grade on an existing slab. The website checklist is available for reference; scanning and saving holdings take place in the Card AI app.
