## Confirm 30C or 30C-CC first

The main 30C set and the 30C-CC Classic Collection are separate catalog groups. A familiar name or artwork is not enough to choose between them. Check the set code and collector number printed on the card, then compare the artwork and finish.

This also keeps the new set separate from Pokemon Celebrations cards released in 2021. Similar anniversary wording does not make two printings interchangeable, and their prices should not be blended.

## Keep raw and graded values separate

A raw copy and a graded copy are different holdings. For raw cards, record the condition you observe. For a slab, record the grading company and grade already printed on it. Card AI does not predict a grade from a photograph.

Available raw and graded prices also depend on the selected record and market coverage. A missing price should not be replaced with a value from a different printing.

## Questions

### Is 30th Celebration the same as the 2021 Celebrations set?

No. Confirm the set code and collector number rather than relying on the anniversary name. This guide covers the ME: 30th Celebration records identified as 30C and 30C-CC in Card AI's catalog on October 10, 2026.

### Does the highest listed price apply to every copy?

No. Each amount belongs to the named card record in the dated snapshot. Printing, finish, language, raw condition, and an existing slab grade can change which available value is relevant.
