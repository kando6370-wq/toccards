Searching for "Minior Pokemon GO" usually means the Pokemon GO encounter event. A Minior caught in the game is not a physical Pokemon card and has no trading card price. If you have a Minior card in hand, identify its **set and collector number** first. That is the useful starting point for a card value check.

## Which Minior card do you have?

The official Pokemon TCG records show more than one Minior printing:

- **Paradox Rift 099/182** is the numbered card in the main set.
- **Paradox Rift 201/182** is an Illustration Rare with a different collector number. Do not use the 099/182 price for it.
- **Sun & Moon—Guardians Rising 77/145** belongs to an earlier expansion. Its set and number distinguish it from either Paradox Rift card.

Check the small collector number on the card before relying on a name-only search. Then confirm the artwork, language, finish and condition. A raw copy and an existing graded slab also need different value records.

## How to check a Minior card's value

Use the [Pokemon card scanner](/pokemon-card-scanner) to get likely catalog matches, or search the catalog by Minior, set and number. Review the matching record yourself. Card AI shows an available published price and history for the selected printing and the raw condition or existing slab grade you enter. Availability of a price or history depends on that record; this guide does not quote a live price or guarantee a sale amount.

Card AI does not authenticate the card, infer the exact printing from the Pokemon name alone, or predict a grade from a photograph. If the number or finish does not match a suggested result, search manually before saving it.

## Pokemon GO Minior versus Pokemon TCG Minior

The [official Pokemon GO Minior announcement](https://pokemongo.com/news/minior-meteor-showers-2026) describes a game encounter. The physical cards above come from separate Pokemon TCG expansions. The Pokemon GO event does not create a new Minior card printing or change a card's set number. For event schedules and game mechanics, use the official Pokemon GO announcement rather than a card price guide.
