Account setup and deletion

Use this page if you cannot access the in-app deletion option or need help deleting a Card AI platform account.

Request deletion by email

Email our support team from the address associated with your Card AI login when possible. Include the platform you want deleted: Android or iOS. We may request only the information needed to verify the account and target platform.

Request deletion by email

What deletion means

  • Android and iOS platform accounts and business data are managed separately.
  • Deleting the requested platform account removes or de-identifies its collections, saved cards, scan records, preferences, and related data from active systems, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • The other platform account and its business data remain available.
  • Old sessions for the deleted platform are revoked.
  • Using Card AI again on that platform requires registering a new platform account. Deleted business data is not restored.

Deleting an iOS Card AI account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel an active subscription through your Apple ID subscription settings.
