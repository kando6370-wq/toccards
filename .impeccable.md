## Design Context

### Users

English-speaking collectors of physical trading cards and sports cards. They use Card AI on mobile while sorting a stack, checking a card in hand, confirming a printing, reviewing a price, or organizing a collection.

### Brand Personality

Accurate, reassuring, and verifiable. Product copy should explain what the app knows, what the user must confirm, and where a price or record may be missing. The page should support editorial and collector references without making unsupported claims.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing Card AI marketing website visual language: dark backgrounds, white text, a lime accent, and the current typography, spacing, navigation, and product proof blocks. Do not introduce a new theme, gradients, or decorative UI for product facts or backlink preparation. The creator confirmed that the visual direction must remain unchanged.

### Design Principles

- Put the collector task and verified product result first.
- Make printing, language, finish, condition, and existing grade easy to understand.
- Use calm hierarchy and readable content blocks for pages that editors can cite.
- Keep app-only actions explicit and keep website content factual.
- Treat missing prices, uncertain matches, and unsupported capabilities as visible product boundaries.
